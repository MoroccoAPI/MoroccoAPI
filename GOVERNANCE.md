# Governance

MoroccoAPI is a community-driven project with transparent decisions and accountable maintainers.

## Roles

### Contributors

Anyone who improves code, data research, documentation, design, testing, or community operations.

### Maintainers

Contributors with a record of consistent, constructive work who can review and merge changes within defined areas.

### Founding maintainer

During incubation, the founding maintainer coordinates releases and may break ties. This temporary authority should be reviewed after the project has at least three active maintainers.

## Decision process

- Routine changes use normal pull-request review.
- API contracts, licenses, governance, and major scope changes require an RFC.
- High-impact changes should receive two maintainer approvals once the project has enough maintainers.
- Decisions and their reasoning must remain publicly accessible.

## Maintainer expectations

- Review contributions respectfully and within a reasonable time.
- Disclose conflicts of interest.
- Protect contributor and user privacy.
- Avoid implying official government endorsement.
- Prefer project sustainability over endpoint count.

## Inactivity

Maintainers who are inactive for an extended period may move to emeritus status after a public, respectful review. They may return through renewed contribution.

## Changes to governance

Governance changes require an RFC and explicit maintainer approval.
