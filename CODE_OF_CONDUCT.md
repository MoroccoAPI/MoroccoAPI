# Code of Conduct

MoroccoAPI is committed to a respectful, inclusive, and technically constructive community.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful and assume good faith while evaluating evidence critically.
- Give specific, actionable feedback.
- Welcome contributors of different experience levels and backgrounds.
- Respect privacy, source licenses, and responsible disclosure.
- Credit the work of contributors and data producers.
- Disclose conflicts of interest.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, threats, or personal attacks.
- Publishing private information without permission.
- Deliberately submitting unlawfully obtained or restricted data.
- Misrepresenting MoroccoAPI as an official government service.
- Repeated spam, bad-faith disruption, or abusive conduct.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, reject, or remove contributions and participation that violate this policy. Serious or repeated violations may result in a temporary or permanent ban.

A private conduct-reporting contact will be published before the community launch. Enforcement decisions should be documented privately and handled consistently.
