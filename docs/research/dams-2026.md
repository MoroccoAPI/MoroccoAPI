# Tensift dam data: 2026 source review

Reviewed on October 16, 2026 for
[issue #33](https://github.com/MoroccoAPI/MoroccoAPI/issues/33).

## Admitted official source

The [data.gov.ma dataset](https://data.gov.ma/data/fr/dataset/situation-journaliere-des-barrages-dans-le-bassin-du-tensift)
is published by the Agence du bassin hydraulique de Tensift (ABHT). Its current
version is September 2026, with catalog coverage from August 1 through September
30, 2026 and metadata last modified October 6, 2026. The
[CKAN package API](https://data.gov.ma/data/api/3/action/package_show?id=situation-journaliere-des-barrages-dans-le-bassin-du-tensift)
lists two resources. A documented subset of catalog metadata is preserved in
`data/dams/raw/catalog-metadata.json`, retaining dataset, license, producer and
resource evidence while omitting contributor contact fields.

| Month | MoroccoAPI source ID | Portal resource ID | Confirmed workbook coverage |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| August | `abht-tensift-2026-08` | `0b69e96f-6c95-40ff-8c91-3721984b2613` | `Août_2026`, August 1–31, 2026 |
| September | `abht-tensift-2026-09` | `57580e59-8b9c-4bde-bf9f-2a25107bbf6e` | `Sept_2026`, September 1–30, 2026 |

Official downloads:

- [August XLSX](https://data.gov.ma/data/fr/dataset/0aff0dd1-3c7e-4e14-9cbf-18782902a6e4/resource/0b69e96f-6c95-40ff-8c91-3721984b2613/download/data_barrages_tensift_aout_2026.xlsx)
- [September XLSX](https://data.gov.ma/data/fr/dataset/0aff0dd1-3c7e-4e14-9cbf-18782902a6e4/resource/57580e59-8b9c-4bde-bf9f-2a25107bbf6e/download/data_barrages_tensift_sept_2026.xlsx)

Inspection of the XLSX contents confirms 2026 observation dates, normal storage
capacities, daily volumes in millions of cubic metres (Mm³), and filling rates
in percent. Five dams have 61 observations each: 305 observations in total.
French source spellings are preserved. Each normalized level records its
source ID, sheet and one-based workbook row; source metadata and SHA-256
checksums are recorded in [`../../sources/dams.yml`](../../sources/dams.yml)
and [`../../data/dams/sources.json`](../../data/dams/sources.json).

The catalog and [resource page](https://data.gov.ma/data/fr/dataset/situation-journaliere-des-barrages-dans-le-bassin-du-tensift/resource/57580e59-8b9c-4bde-bf9f-2a25107bbf6e?inner_span=True)
explicitly identify Open Data Commons Open Database License (ODbL). The saved
catalog metadata reports `license_id: odc-odbl`, `isopen: true`, and
`license_url: https://opendefinition.org/licenses/odc-odbl`. The adapted database
is published under [ODbL 1.0](https://opendatacommons.org/licenses/odbl/1-0/),
with attribution in [the dataset attribution](../../data/dams/ATTRIBUTION.md).

## Search for other 2026 data

French and Arabic searches covered data.gov.ma, ABHT, the equipment/water
ministry and Maadialna. Queries included Tensift plus 2026, daily dam situation,
XLSX filenames, and January/July/October variants. No additional reusable monthly
Tensift workbook beyond September was found in this review. That is a search
finding, not a claim that no other measurements exist.

The [ABHT homepage](https://abht.ma/) displays a newer snapshot dated October 13,
2026, covering seven listed dams. Its footer reserves rights, and this review
did not identify an open-data license for that website snapshot. Those readings
are excluded from the published database. Individual dam pages can be stale:
the [Lalla Takerkoust detail page](https://abht.ma/Barrage/lalla-takerkoust/)
showed an undated value different from the dated homepage.

ABHT's [water-resource situation page](https://abht.ma/bassin/situation-ressources-en-eau/)
has a 2026 modification date but links a 2022–2023 hydrological report. A recent
[Maadialna ministry article](https://www.maadialna.ma/ar/bartfa-ynahz-166-mqarnt-m-alsnt-almadyt-hwd-tansyft-ysjl-wardat-mayyt-tblgh-305-mlywn-mtr-mkb-khlal),
published October 13, 2026, reports accumulated inflows from September 2025
through January 12, 2026. Neither provides a later monthly set of daily stored
water volumes suitable for extending this snapshot. Publication and webpage
modification dates must be distinguished from observation dates.

## Coverage and reproducibility

The API covers the five dams in these two files, not all Moroccan dams or all
of 2026. Its `latest_level` means September 30, 2026 in the current snapshot,
not a live reading. Missing dates are not interpolated. Portal resource IDs
have changed during file replacements; pinned original files and checksums
make the published snapshot reproducible independently of later portal edits.

Run `python scripts/dams/pipeline.py --check` to compare the normalized outputs
with the pinned original workbooks. See [the dataset and API guide](../../data/dams/README.md)
for endpoint fields and filter behavior.
